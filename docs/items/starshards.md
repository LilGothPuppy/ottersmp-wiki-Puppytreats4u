# Star Shards/Seasonal currency

Previously known as Star Shards, now known as seasonal currency, are a form of common [currency](../Currency.md) found on OtterSMP.

The main use of seasonal currency is within the [Starfox](../vendors/starfox.md) shop.
## Obtaining Seasonal currency

Seasonal currency is a very easy thing to obtain. Because of this, items at [Starfox](../vendors/Starfox.md) are set at different prices, with some rarer items costing over 1000 to purchase.

### Missions
The most popular and easiest way to obtain seasonal currency is found within the [/missions](../commands/basic.md) menu. Completing daily and weekly missions for each of our [NPCS]()to obtain seasonal currency and other items.

### Voting

The second most common method of obtaining seasonal currency is by voting; the only downside is you can only vote for a server once a day. (You can vote on all 8 links once every 24 hours to obtain goodies from voting)

### Dungeons

The third common method of obtaining Seasonal currency is by repeatedly running [dungeons](../Dungeons.md) over and over again. While yes, this method may be repetitive, the grind makes it worth the time sink. On average, one can expect around a stack of seasonal currency per dungeon run.

### Milestones


A not-so-popular method of acquiring seasonal currency is by utilising the [milestones](../Milestones.md) feature. The reason why I say this is such an unpopular route is due to the alternative rewards the player can choose instead. While yes, a lot of seasonal currency is up for grabs if anyone really wants it, the other options include keys, armor sets, and more.

### Trading or Purchasing from other players

Lastly, players can trade with other players to acquire seasonal currency. Utilising things like the stall feature at spawn or simply peer-to-peer trading for items allows seasonal currency to circulate throughout the world.
